How this is calculated

One offer in detail: what you pay upfront, every month, and over the whole term. Pick the offer in the Scenario panel or click it in the Offers table. Your income, house price and down payment drive the Offers table too.

The “optimum” button sets price = your income ceiling ÷ the bank’s % of price: the last price at which the down payment is still the bank’s minimum. Above it the loan stays at the ceiling and every extra euro of price is ours. Formulas: loan = min(price − down payment, bank % × price, income ceiling); annuity payment at the bank’s rate (fixed period per offer: 24, 36 or 60 months, or the whole term; then Euribor 6M 2,46 % + spread); IMT = base × rate − abatimento. Checked against the broker’s comparison at 585 000 € and 400 000 €: Eurobic mixed 1 473,22 €, Santander 1 462,34 €, CGD 1 611,86 €, Bankinter fixed 1 832,98 € — all match; IMT 33 036,65 € + stamp duties 7 080,00 € + each bank’s fees reproduce its “total costs including taxes” to the cent.

Assumptions

Same house, bank and down payment as in the Scenario panel. The renter keeps the same money that would have gone into the deal and each month sets aside the difference between the owner’s costs and the rent. The answer depends more on the horizon, house-price growth and the return on savings than on the bank’s terms — move them.

Assumptions: both scenarios spend the same — the renter keeps the same cash that would have gone into the deal and each month sets aside (or draws down) the difference between the owner’s costs (payment + insurance + upkeep) and the rent. Loan principal is not a cost but savings held in the walls; the owner’s costs are interest, insurance, upkeep and the forgone return on the cash. Upkeep is 1,2 % of the house’s current value per year (repairs ~1 % + IMI ~0,2 %). At the end of the horizon the buyer sells: minus agent 5 % + IVA (6,15 % of price) and a 0,5 % early-repayment fee on the balance. Not included: tax on investment income (IRS 28 %) and capital-gains tax on the sale (mais-valias) — none if the money goes into another primary home, otherwise half the gain is taxed under IRS. Rate after the fixed period = current Euribor; everything in nominal terms. Default rent: 1 600 €/month, roughly what a comparable home rents for.

Deal roadmap

The path from a house we like to the keys. The order fits our situation: we already have an approval letter without a property (Eurobic), so the bank steps are short, and the promissory contract (CPCV) moves to the end or drops out. Timings are typical for the market.

Offer and reservation (proposta / ficha de reserva) seller replies in 1–5 days

A written price offer through the agency. Often this is a reservation form: we pay 1 000–5 000 € as a deposit to the agency (not the seller); once the seller accepts, the property comes off the market. The amount counts towards the price. The key clause: if the bank doesn’t lend, the money is returned. Before signing, write in a realistic deadline to the CPCV/escritura — 30–45 days, with room for the bank’s cycle.

The reservation form isn’t mandatory. It’s agency practice, not a legal requirement: an offer can be made by a simple letter (proposta de compra) with no money. The amount and terms are negotiable — you can offer 500–1 000 € and add your own conditions (bank valuation not below X, survey). But the reservation also works for us: once accepted, the seller is locked in and can’t sell to someone else during the bank’s cycle.

~1–2 weeksCollecting the property documents: tax record (caderneta predial), land-registry extract (certidão predial), occupancy licence (licença de utilização), energy certificate. The package goes via the broker to the bank, and the bank orders the valuation (usually 0–350 €, often waived in campaigns).
in parallel · 7–10 daysOur own due diligence, as the property requires: structural engineer (damp, roof, cracks, electrics, ~150–400 €), lawyer for the paperwork (charges on the certidão, licença, condominium debts), architect if a rebuild is planned. Add the right to withdraw based on the inspections as a separate condition in the reservation (step 1). All of this strictly before the CPCV: after it, hidden defects are our problem.

Property valuation (avaliação) visit + report 3–7 days

The bank’s valuer inspects the house and writes a report. The loan is limited to a share of the lower of price and valuation — at 400 000 € on 585 000 € we are at 68 %, well under the usual 80–90 % limits, so a valuation up to ~15 % below the price still works.

Low valuation = exit without loss. A valuation below the threshold cuts the loan, and an insufficient loan should be covered by the reservation’s financing clause. To be safe, spell it out when signing: “if the bank valuation is below X € or the approved loan is below Y € — withdrawal with a full refund of the reservation”.

3–10 working daysThe bank checks the valuation and property documents and issues the final decision for the specific property + a contract offer.

Final approval for the property reflection period 7–30 days

The bank confirms the loan for this house and sends the contract offer. By law there’s a minimum of 7 days to consider it and a maximum of 30. From this point the money is guaranteed.

CPCV (promissory contract with deposit) floating step

The deposit (sinal) of ~10 % now goes to the seller. The market classic is to sign right after the offer, before the bank: then a financing condition (condição suspensiva) is essential, otherwise the deposit is lost if the bank refuses. Our route: after final approval (almost no risk), or skip it entirely and go straight to the escritura. Cost of backing out: the buyer loses the deposit, the seller repays double, and for housing a court can enforce the sale (execução específica).

2–6 weeksPreparing the escritura: a date with the notary, the bank prepares the mortgage deed, the seller pays off their own mortgage (distrate). We gather the cash for the deal: down payment + taxes and fees (~42 000 € in costs on a 400 000 € loan) — IMT and stamp duty are paid before signing.

Escritura (escritura + mortgage deed) 1 day

At the notary: signatures, the bank releases the loan, the seller gets paid, we get the keys. Ownership transfers only here. The bank registers the purchase and the mortgage (already in its costs).

In total, from offer acceptance to keys is usually 1,5–3 months (minimum ~1 month with documents ready and no CPCV, maximum ~4 with a seller chain and a slow bank). The payment schedule by stage is in “When the money goes out” in Breakdown.

How to compare offers

The headline rate is misleading: a bank can offer a low rate and make it back through insurance and fees. Compare loans at the same amount and term, using these four figures — all of them are in the bank’s standard offer.

Offer

FINE in Portuguese (ESIS in English): the standard EU mortgage offer every bank must give you, with rate, TAEG, payment, fees and total cost in the same format.

TAN

Nominal annual rate — the loan’s interest rate on its own, without costs.

TAEG

Effective annual rate: TAN + mandatory insurance, fees and taxes. The main figure for comparing banks.

Prestação

Monthly payment. Handy for “can we afford it?”, but banks can only be compared on it at equal amount and term.

MTIC

Everything you pay the bank over the whole term: principal + interest + costs. The loan’s total price in one number.
